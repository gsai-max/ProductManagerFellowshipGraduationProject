## 1. Solution Exploration Trade-Off Matrix

| Pain Point (Research Finding) | Solution | What it Solves | Pros | Limitation | Why This Solution? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fixed Shopping Mission:** Users open Blinkit with a fixed mission and ignore other categories. | **AI Mission Intelligence Platform (MIP)** | Identifies shopping mission, predicts adjacent intent, and orchestrates personalized interventions | Solves the root cause (confidence + context), scalable across all touchpoints | Higher initial AI investment | Solves the root cause by understanding *why* the customer is shopping before deciding *what* to recommend. |

## 2. Selected Solution: AI Mission Intelligence Platform (MIP)

### Why This Solution?
Rather than recommending products based only on purchase history, the platform first understands:
1. **Shopping Mission:** Why is the user here today? (e.g. Weekly replenishment, emergency tech fix, late-night snack)
2. **Household Context:** Family, pet owner, bachelor, etc.
3. **Purchase Behavior:** Routine buyer vs. exploratory buyer
4. **Current Basket Content:** Real-time item intent tokens
5. **Temporal Context:** Weekend, festival, late night, guests at home
6. **Confidence Score:** Likelihood to try something new

### Activation Touchpoints
The platform becomes the central intelligence layer activating personalized interventions across:
- **Homepage:** Dynamic Category Ribbon
- **Search:** Contextual Synonym & Cross-Category Boost
- **Checkout:** One-Choice Micro-Trial Prompt
- **Push Notifications & CRM:** Occasion-led nudges
- **Future AI Shopping Assistant:** Interactive voice/chat discovery

### Expected Outcomes

| Product Outcomes | Business Outcomes |
| :--- | :--- |
| $\uparrow$ Adjacent Category Discovery | $\uparrow$ Cross-Category GMV |
| $\uparrow$ Recommendation Acceptance | $\uparrow$ Average Basket Value (AOV) |
| $\uparrow$ First-Time Category Purchase | $\uparrow$ Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) |
| $\uparrow$ Repeat Cross-Category Shopping | $\uparrow$ Revenue Diversification |
| $\uparrow$ User Confidence Score | $\uparrow$ MAC buying $\ge 1$ New Category / Month |

## 3. Activation Channels vs. Competing Solutions

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Strategic Insight:** Homepage Personalization, Checkout Prompts, Trial Packs, and Voucher Engines are **not competing solutions**—they are **activation channels**. The Mission Intelligence Platform is the foundational intelligence layer powering all of them.

- **Why Not Homepage Personalization First?** Optimizes exposure but not behavior. Users still ignore unfamiliar categories without confidence.
- **Why Not Checkout Prompts First?** Excellent activation channel, but depends on intelligent intent prediction first.
- **Why Not Discovery Trial Packs First?** Useful behavioral nudge but category-specific and difficult to scale independently.
- **Why Not Discount Engine First?** Creates short-term transactions rather than long-term habit formation.

## 5. What Changes the Ranking? (RICE Sensitivity Triggers)

| Strategic Trigger | Ranking Shift | Why? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **New users become primary growth objective** | Dynamic Homepage Personalization $\uparrow$ | Discovery at top of funnel becomes more important than mission intelligence for existing users. |
| **Cross-category conversion is below target despite good discovery** | Smart Checkout Prompts $\uparrow$ | Checkout becomes highest-leverage intervention point for influencing purchase decisions. |
| **High price sensitivity or low trust in specific category** | Adaptive Trust & Incentive Engine $\uparrow$ | Trust-building and targeted incentives become more valuable than additional recommendations. |
| **Launching new strategic categories (Beauty, Pet Care, Electronics)** | Explorer Trial Packs $\uparrow$ | Trial bundles accelerate first-time adoption in categories with higher perceived risk. |
| **Long-term platform investment is prioritized** | Mission Intelligence Engine Remains #1 | Scales across all customer touchpoints and powers every downstream activation. |
